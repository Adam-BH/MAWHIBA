## Notes for recording a voice-over
- About 150 words; read at a relaxed 130–140 words per minute and it lands inside each scene.
- Duck the music by about 8 dB under the VO; the SFX can stay as they are.
- If you go French for a local pitch, keep the same timings: each line has about 0.5 s of slack.
